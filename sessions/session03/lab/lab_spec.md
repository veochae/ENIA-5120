# Lab 03: EV Infrastructure Audit

## Goals
- Load, clean, and type-check EV population and charging station datasets
- Compare demand vs supply at the city and county level
- Practice joins and ratios to identify infrastructure gaps
- Build clear visual diagnostics (bars, boxplots, maps)

## Tasks
1. Load `wa_ev_population.csv` and `wa_ev_charge_stations.csv`.
2. Clean strings and recode eligibility; standardize ZIP/postal codes.
3. Compute EV counts by city and visualize the top 15.
4. Compute station counts by city and visualize the top 15.
5. Compare left/inner/full joins and build a city audit with EV-to-station ratios.
6. Compute county-level EV/station ratios and plot them.
7. Compare station groups (Public vs Private) across top counties.
8. Pivot charger tech levels and compare distributions with a boxplot.
9. Map EV density vs station locations across Washington.
