\hypertarget{mean-vs.-median-numeric}{%
\subsubsection{3.1 Mean vs.~Median
(Numeric)}\label{mean-vs.-median-numeric}}

\textbf{functions:}\\
\texttt{filter(!is.na(x))}: Deletion tool that drops missing rows.\\
\texttt{mean()} / \texttt{median()}: Measures central tendency;
\texttt{na.rm\ =\ TRUE} ignores NAs.\\
\texttt{coalesce()}: Imputation tool that fills NAs with a replacement
value.\\
\texttt{sd()}: Calculates Standard Deviation (spread).
\texttt{mutate()}: Adds or replaces a column in the data frame.

\textbf{logic steps:}\\
Create a deletion-only version by removing missing
\texttt{crew\_size}.\\
Compute the mean and median to see if they diverge.\\
Create two imputed versions using mean and median.\\
Compare resulting mean and SD across strategies to judge impact.
