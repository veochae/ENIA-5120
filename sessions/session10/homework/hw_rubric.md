# Homework 04 Rubric

**Total Points:** 30

## 1. Question and Method Fit (6 points)
States a clear analytical question, chooses a modeling method that fits the question, and justifies the variable selection.

- **6**: Question is clear and substantive; method choice is well justified; variables and setup strongly match the modeling goal.
- **4**: Question and method mostly fit, with minor gaps in justification or variable choice.
- **2**: Basic question is present, but the model choice or variable selection is only partly justified.
- **0**: Question, method choice, or variable logic is unclear or missing.

## 2. Model Construction and Selection (8 points)
Builds a reasonable modeling workflow, compares alternatives when appropriate, and explains why the final model or clustering setup was chosen.

- **8**: Modeling workflow is careful and well defended; alternative models or settings are compared meaningfully; final choice is clearly justified.
- **6**: Workflow is good overall, with minor gaps in comparison or model-selection reasoning.
- **4**: A model is fit correctly, but comparison or justification of the final setup is limited.
- **2**: Modeling workflow is weak, incomplete, or only partly appropriate for the method.
- **0**: No meaningful modeling workflow shown.

## 3. Result Interpretation (10 points)
Explains the most important model outputs correctly in plain language, including variable impact or cluster characteristics.

- **10**: Interpretation is accurate, specific, and substantive; clearly explains what matters in the results and what was found to be true.
- **8**: Strong interpretation with only minor gaps in depth, precision, or clarity.
- **5**: Key outputs are reported, but explanation of their meaning is limited or partly mechanical.
- **2**: Interpretation is weak, vague, or partially incorrect.
- **0**: Results are missing or not meaningfully interpreted.

## 4. Evidence and Evaluation (4 points)
Includes appropriate diagnostics, evaluation metrics, visuals, or cluster summaries for the chosen method.

- **4**: Uses method-appropriate evidence well, such as test metrics, confidence intervals, confusion matrix, elbow plot, dendrogram, or cluster summaries.
- **3**: Includes mostly appropriate evidence with minor omissions.
- **2**: Some supporting evidence is present, but important diagnostics or summaries are missing.
- **1**: Very limited supporting evidence.
- **0**: No meaningful supporting evidence.

## 5. Communication and Caution (2 points)
Writing is clear, well-organized, and includes appropriate limitations or cautions.

- **2**: Writing is clear and well structured, and the analysis includes appropriate caution about limits or assumptions.
- **1**: Writing is understandable but uneven, brief, or light on limitations.
- **0**: Writing is unclear or lacks meaningful caution.
