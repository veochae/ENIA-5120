# Homework 05 – Executive Summary & Storytelling

Deliver a polished 2–3 page executive summary (plus optional one-slide visual) that:

1. States the problem, briefly describes the data sources, and highlights the main findings from your analyses/models.
2. Presents 2–3 key visuals and/or tables with plain-language explanations suitable for non-technical stakeholders.
3. Summarizes model results at a high level (what worked, what metrics matter) without deep math.
4. Provides actionable recommendations and acknowledges limitations or open questions.
